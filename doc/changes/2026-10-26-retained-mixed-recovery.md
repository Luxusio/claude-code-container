# Retain mutually blocking predecessor responsibilities before cleanup

A pending predecessor can veto another predecessor's shutdown without accepting its cleanup obligation. CCC now qualifies and retains a compatible cohort before cleanup, then permits restricted access to the same verified existing container. The new session gains no cleanup authority; old records and vetoes remain protective.

Original predecessor receipts remain pinned across admission. Changed or unqualified evidence and actual cleanup failures remain errors; bounded cause codes distinguish those failures from policy deferral. This does not settle historical obligations or prove actual macOS acceptance.

See [retained predecessor admission](../session/REQ__retained-predecessor-session-admission.md).
